Privacy notice

← Return to your home file

Effective August 28, 2026. Home Maintenance Receipts is designed so the operator does not receive your home-maintenance records.

Records and attachments

Records, settings, and evidence files you add are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB on this device. They are not uploaded to us. Clearing browser storage or removing the app may delete them. Use the full JSON backup regularly and store that copy somewhere you control.

Exports

PDF, CSV, and JSON files are created on your device. Once downloaded, those files are governed by the privacy and security of the location where you save or share them.

Purchase information

If you purchase House File Plus, checkout and license verification are provided by Sociobot and Dodo, the merchant of record. They receive the information necessary to process the transaction. This app stores your license token and a cached verification result in local browser storage. The token is sent to the Sociobot verification endpoint no more than once per day.

Network and analytics

The app contains no advertising trackers, third-party fonts, or behavioral analytics. Standard hosting and billing server logs may include limited technical data such as an IP address, user agent, time, and requested URL for security and operations.

Your choices

You can export your records at any time. You can delete individual records in the app. To erase everything, clear this site’s browser storage after first saving any backup you want to keep.

Contact

For privacy requests about the hosted product, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
