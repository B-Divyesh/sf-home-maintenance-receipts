Privacy

Privacy notice

Effective September 6, 2026. The operator does not receive the home-maintenance records you enter.

Records and attachments

Records, settings, and evidence files are stored in this browser’s IndexedDB. They are not uploaded to us. Clearing browser storage or removing the app may delete them. Download a full JSON backup and keep it somewhere you control.

Sample demo

The demo uses a separate IndexedDB database whose name starts with demo:. It does not read or change your real home record. Starting a real record deletes the demo database.

Exports

PDF, CSV, and JSON files are created on your device. The location where you save or share an export controls its later privacy.

Purchase information

The app contacts Sociobot when it checks checkout availability or verifies a license. Sociobot and Dodo process purchase information as merchant of record. This app stores your license token and a cached result in localStorage. It verifies an active token at most once per day.

Network and analytics

The app contains no advertising trackers, remote fonts, CDN scripts, or behavioral analytics. Hosting and billing logs may contain an IP address, browser details, time, and requested URL for security and operations.

Your choices

You can export records and delete individual entries. To erase everything, save any backup you want and then clear this site’s browser storage.

Contact

Send privacy requests to privacy@sociobot.in.
